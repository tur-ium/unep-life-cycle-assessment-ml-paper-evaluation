**Analytical Framework to Resolve Discrepancies in Plant-Based Meat LCAs**

**1. Methodological Differences**  
- **Data Sources**: Compare primary data (e.g., industry-specific) vs. secondary/generic data (e.g., databases like Ecoinvent). Inconsistent data quality or age can skew results (Poore & Nemecek, 2018).  
- **System Boundaries**: Assess inclusions (e.g., farming, processing, packaging, transport) and exclusions (e.g., land-use change, refrigeration). Narrow boundaries may understate impacts (UNEP, 2020).  
- **Functional Units**: Check if units align (e.g., per kg product vs. per kg protein). Protein-adjusted comparisons often favor plant-based options (Heller & Keoleian, 2018).  
- **LCIA Methods**: Evaluate impact categories (e.g., GHG emissions vs. water use) and characterization models (e.g., IPCC vs. ReCiPe). Disparate weighting of impacts (e.g., carbon vs. biodiversity) drives divergence (Clune et al., 2017).  

**2. Harmonization Steps**  
- **Reconcile Functional Units**: Convert results to a common basis (e.g., protein content) to enable direct comparison.  
- **Adjust System Boundaries**: Exclude stages not covered in both studies (e.g., consumer cooking) or recalculate impacts using shared boundaries.  
- **Sensitivity Analysis**: Test how assumptions (e.g., energy mix, yield variability) affect outcomes. High sensitivity suggests lower reliability.  
- **Uncertainty Assessment**: Prioritize studies quantifying data uncertainty (e.g., Monte Carlo analysis) or using peer-reviewed inventories (ISO 14044, 2006).  
- **Contextualize Scope**: If one study focuses solely on GHG emissions while another includes broader impacts (e.g., eutrophication), synthesize findings thematically.  

**3. Recommendations for Future LCAs**  
- **Standardize Functional Units**: Use nutritionally relevant metrics (e.g., protein/calorie equivalence) (Willett et al., 2019).  
- **Adopt Tiered Boundaries**: Define core (e.g., production) and optional (e.g., transport) modules per ISO 14044 to enhance comparability.  
- **Transparent Reporting**: Disclose data sources, allocation methods, and cut-off criteria to enable replication.  
- **Multi-Method LCIA**: Apply complementary impact assessment models (e.g., IPCC and AWARE for water) to capture trade-offs.  
- **Third-Peer Review**: Ensure independent verification, particularly for industry-funded studies, to mitigate bias.  

**Key Citations**: Poore & Nemecek (2018)*Science*, Heller & Keoleian (2018)*ES&T*, UNEP (2020), ISO 14044 (2006).  

This framework prioritizes methodological transparency and quantitative harmonization to resolve conflicts, emphasizing robustness in data and alignment with LCA best practices.
