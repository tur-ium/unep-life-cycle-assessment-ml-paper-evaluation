The question that can be best answered using life cycle assessment (LCA) as defined in the ISO 14044 standard is:

- **C: What is the impact of using a new synthesis route for a feedstock chemical on the carbon footprint of a plastic sheet?**

Life cycle assessment is a methodology for assessing the environmental impacts associated with all stages of a product's life from cradle to grave (i.e., from raw material extraction through materials processing, manufacture, distribution, use, repair and maintenance, and disposal or recycling). ISO 14044 provides the principles and framework for LCA. Question C directly pertains to evaluating the environmental impact (specifically the carbon footprint) of a change in the production process of a product, which is a classic application of LCA.

The other options do not fit the scope of LCA as defined by ISO 14044:
- **A** involves policy comparison and environmental management, not a product life cycle.
- **B** focuses on health outcomes rather than environmental impacts.
- **D** is about biological life cycles and pest management, not the environmental assessment of a product's life cycle.
