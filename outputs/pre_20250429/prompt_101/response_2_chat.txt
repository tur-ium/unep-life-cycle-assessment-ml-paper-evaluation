Okay, here is a structured recommendation for selecting the appropriate LCA methodology:

The choice between attributional (ALCA) and consequential (CLCA) LCA depends critically on the study's goal and the decisions it aims to inform.

**1. Pilot-scale Demonstration Phase:**

*   **Recommended Approach:** Attributional LCA (ALCA).
*   **Goal & Scope:** To understand the environmental profile and potential hotspots of the *novel process itself*, independent of large-scale market effects. Scope should focus on cradle-to-gate (hydrogen at plant gate), detailing the specific inputs (solar panels, electrolyzer, water) and outputs of the pilot facility.
*   **Data Collection:** Prioritize primary data from the pilot plant operations. Use average data for background processes (e.g., manufacturing of components) as market impacts are negligible at this scale.
*   **Justification:** The primary goal is internal process optimization, feasibility assessment, and identifying R&D priorities. Stakeholders (R&D team, internal management) need accurate data on the *current* process performance, not hypothetical market shifts. ALCA provides a clear environmental snapshot of the technology (Curran, 2015). Consequences of this phase relate to technology refinement, not broad economic changes.

**2. Large-scale Commercial Deployment (Renewable-Dominated Grid):**

*   **Recommended Approach:** Consequential LCA (CLCA).
*   **Goal & Scope:** To assess the *system-wide environmental consequences* of introducing this technology at scale, informing investment and policy decisions. Scope must include market-mediated effects: identifying the *marginal* electricity source(s) affected (even in a renewable grid, this could involve changes in storage, curtailment, or displacement of other demands/sources) and potential displacement of existing hydrogen production.
*   **Data Collection:** Focus on identifying and modelling the marginal suppliers/technologies likely affected by the increased demand for electricity and supply of hydrogen. Requires market analysis alongside process data.
