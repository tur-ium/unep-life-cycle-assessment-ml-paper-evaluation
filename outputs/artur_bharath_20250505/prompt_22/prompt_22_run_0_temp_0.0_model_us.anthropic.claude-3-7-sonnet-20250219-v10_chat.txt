# Environmental Comparison of Products A and B

To determine which product is more environmentally friendly, I need to compare their impacts across multiple environmental indicators:

## Comparison of Environmental Impacts

| Impact Category | Product A | Product B | Difference |
|----------------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Carbon Footprint | 5 kg CO2 eq | 2 kg CO2 eq | Product B is 60% lower |
| Water Footprint | 10 m³ deprived | 30 m³ deprived | Product A is 67% lower |
| Marine Eutrophication | 3 kg N eq | 3 kg N eq | Equal impact |

## Analysis

This comparison presents a trade-off:
- Product B has a significantly lower carbon footprint (climate change impact)
- Product A has a substantially lower water footprint (water resource depletion)
- Both products have identical marine eutrophication impacts

Without knowing the specific context (such as where these products are used, whether water scarcity is a local concern, or which environmental impact is prioritized), it's difficult to make a definitive determination of which is "more environmentally friendly" overall.

The better choice would depend on whether climate change mitigation or water conservation is the higher priority in the specific usage context.
