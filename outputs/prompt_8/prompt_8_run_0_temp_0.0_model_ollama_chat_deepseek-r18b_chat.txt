### Methodological Strategy for LCA Comparing Battery Recycling vs. Reuse in Energy Storage Applications

#### 1. **Introduction**
This study aims to conduct a Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) comparing the environmental impacts of battery recycling versus battery reuse in energy storage applications. Given the lack of direct Life Cycle Inventory (LCI) data for reuse scenarios and the rapid evolution of recycling technologies, this analysis will employ a systematic approach to address uncertainties and provide clear boundaries, functional units, and assumptions.

#### 2. **Handling Data Uncertainty and Technological Variability**
Data uncertainty is a critical challenge, particularly for reuse scenarios where no direct LCI data are available (Cui et al., 2020). To mitigate this, the study will rely on secondary data sources, such as literature reviews, industry reports, and expert opinions. For recycling technologies, which are rapidly evolving, the analysis will incorporate sensitivity analyses to assess how variations in key parameters (e.g., energy recovery efficiency, emissions from downstream processes) affect the overall environmental impact.

#### 3. **Setting Boundaries and Choosing an Appropriate Functional Unit**
- **Boundaries:** The system boundary for recycling will be defined as "cradle-to-grave," encompassing raw material extraction, battery production, use in energy storage, and final disposal. For reuse, the boundary will extend from the second-life application (e.g., remanufacturing or refurbishing) back into the supply chain.
  
- **Functional Unit:** The functional unit for recycling will be defined as "one battery cycle," representing the production, use, and disposal of a standard battery. For reuse, the functional unit will be "one battery in its second-life application," considering the energy storage service provided by the reused battery.

#### 4. **Justifying Methodological Assumptions to Stakeholders**
To justify assumptions to stakeholders unfamiliar with LCA methods:
- **Battery Technology Focus:** The analysis will focus on a specific battery technology (e.g., lithium-ion) that is widely used in energy storage applications, as this provides a clear and relevant comparison.
  
- **Data Sources for Reuse Scenarios:** Since no direct LCI data are available for reuse, the study will rely on secondary data from studies that have analyzed similar scenarios (e.g., Zhang et al., 2019). This approach is justified by the lack of primary data and the need to extrapolate from existing research.

- **Recycling Technology Evolution:** The rapid evolution of recycling technologies justifies the inclusion of sensitivity analyses, which allow for a range of plausible outcomes based on current knowledge.
