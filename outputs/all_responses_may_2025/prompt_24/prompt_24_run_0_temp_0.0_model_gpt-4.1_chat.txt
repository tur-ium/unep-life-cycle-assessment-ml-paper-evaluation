To conduct an LCA for a crop from a regenerative farm, you need to collect:

**1. Goal & Scope Definition:**  
- Functional unit (e.g., 1 kg of crop at farm gate)  
- System boundaries (e.g., cradle-to-farm gate)

**2. Inventory Data (Life Cycle Inventory):**  
- **Inputs:**  
  - Seeds (type, quantity, source)  
  - Water use (irrigation volume, source)  
  - Fertilizers (type, amount, origin—especially organic/compost)  
  - Pesticides/herbicides (type, amount, frequency)  
  - Energy use (fuel/electricity for machinery, irrigation, etc.)  
  - Machinery (type, hours used, maintenance)  
  - Labor (hours, practices)

- **Regenerative Practices:**  
  - Cover cropping (species, area, frequency)  
  - Crop rotation details  
  - Compost/manure application (type, amount, source)  
  - Reduced/no-till practices  
  - Agroforestry or integration of livestock (if applicable)  
  - Soil amendments (biochar, etc.)

- **Outputs:**  
  - Crop yield (amount, quality)  
  - Co-products or by-products  
  - Emissions (N₂O, CH₄, CO₂ from soil, compost, livestock)  
  - Soil carbon sequestration data (if available)

- **Waste Management:**  
  - Crop residues (management, fate)  
  - On-farm waste (handling, disposal)

**3. Site-Specific Data:**  
- Soil type, climate, field size  
- Baseline soil carbon (if assessing sequestration)

**4. Transport:**  
- Distance and mode for input delivery and crop distribution (if included in scope)

Collecting accurate, site-specific data is crucial for a robust LCA of regenerative agriculture.
