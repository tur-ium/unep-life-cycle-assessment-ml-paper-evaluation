This BoM details the major components of a toothbrush and their associated environmental impacts during production.  It includes estimated weight, energy and water use, and emissions to air, water, and soil. The processes cover raw material extraction, manufacturing, assembly, and transport. The raw material rows include the cradle-to-gate emissions of their component. Uncertainties are included to reflect the variability in production processes and data sources.
