To guide the energy company in selecting the appropriate life cycle assessment (LCA) approach, goal, scope, and data collection methodology, consider the following recommendations for each scenario:

### Pilot-scale Demonstration Phase
**Goal and Scope:**
- **Goal:** To evaluate the environmental performance of the novel hydrogen production process in a controlled setting.
- **Scope:** Focus on the direct impacts of the pilot-scale process, including material and energy inputs, emissions, and waste outputs.

**Methodological Choice:**
- **Attributional LCA (ALCA):** This approach is suitable for the pilot phase as it provides a snapshot of the current environmental impacts without considering future market changes (Curran et al., 2005). It helps identify hotspots and areas for improvement within the process itself.

**Data Collection:**
- **Primary Data:** Collect detailed data on material and energy inputs, emissions, and waste outputs directly from the pilot plant.
- **Secondary Data:** Use databases like Ecoinvent for background processes (e.g., material production) to complement primary data.

### Large-scale Commercial Deployment in an Electricity Grid Dominated by Renewable Energy
**Goal and Scope:**
- **Goal:** To assess the broader environmental impacts of scaling up the hydrogen production process within a renewable-dominated grid.
- **Scope:** Include direct and indirect impacts, considering the interaction with the electricity grid and potential market responses.

**Methodological Choice:**
- **Consequential LCA (CLCA):** This approach is appropriate as it considers the system-wide effects of large-scale deployment, including changes in the electricity market and potential displacement of other energy sources (Ekvall & Weidema, 2004).

**Data Collection:**
- **Primary Data:** Collect data on the scaled-up process, including energy consumption and emissions.
- **Secondary Data:** Use market data and scenarios to model the impacts on the electricity grid and other affected markets. Utilize databases like GREET for transportation-related impacts.

### Large-scale Deployment in a Grid Dominated by Fossil-based Electricity
**Goal and Scope:**
- **Goal:** To evaluate the environmental consequences of deploying the hydrogen production process in a fossil-dominated grid, focusing on potential reductions in greenhouse gas emissions.
- **Scope:** Consider the direct impacts of the process and the indirect impacts on the electricity market, including potential displacement of fossil fuels.

**Methodological Choice:**
- **Consequential LCA (CLCA):** This approach is essential to capture the system-wide effects, particularly the potential for reducing fossil fuel use and associated emissions (Weidema, 2003).

**Data Collection:**
- **Primary Data:** Collect data on the large-scale process, including energy consumption and emissions.
- **Secondary Data:** Use market data and scenarios to model the impacts on the electricity grid and other affected markets. Utilize databases like Ecoinvent and GREET for comprehensive impact assessment.

### Justification:
- **Pilot Phase:** ALCA is justified as it provides a clear understanding of the process's current environmental performance, which is crucial for stakeholders interested in immediate improvements and feasibility.
- **Renewable Grid:** CLCA is justified to capture the broader impacts of integrating the process into a renewable-dominated grid, addressing stakeholder interests in system-wide sustainability.
- **Fossil Grid:** CLCA is justified to assess the potential for significant emission reductions, aligning with stakeholder interests in mitigating climate change impacts.
