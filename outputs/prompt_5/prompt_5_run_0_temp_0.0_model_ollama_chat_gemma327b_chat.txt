The correct answer is **A: 1. Avoid allocation as far as possible, 2. Allocation on basis of physical properties, 3. Allocation on basis of revenue**

Here's why:

ISO 14044 (and reflected in ISO 15804:2012+A2:2019) prioritizes avoiding allocation altogether. This is because allocation introduces uncertainty and subjectivity into the LCA. 

*   **Avoiding allocation** (through system boundary adjustments like subdivision or expansion) is the most desirable approach as it provides the most accurate and transparent results.
*   If allocation *cannot* be avoided, **allocation based on physical relationships** (e.g., mass, energy) is preferred over economic allocation. Physical relationships are generally considered more objective and representative of the actual process.
*   **Allocation based on economic value (revenue)** is the least preferred method because it's susceptible to market fluctuations and doesn't necessarily reflect the environmental burden of producing each product.

Therefore, option A accurately reflects the hierarchy outlined in the ISO standards.
