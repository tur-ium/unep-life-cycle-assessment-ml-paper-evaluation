To conduct an LCA for a crop from a regenerative farm, gather:

1. **Input Data**: Quantities of seeds, organic fertilizers, compost, biopesticides, water usage, and energy consumption.

2. **Soil Health Metrics**: Soil organic carbon levels, microbial activity, nutrient content, and erosion rates.

3. **Crop Yield**: Total harvest weight and quality metrics.

4. **Machinery Use**: Types, fuel consumption, hours of operation, and maintenance records.

5. **Labor Inputs**: Hours worked and activities performed.

6. **Emissions Data**: Greenhouse gas emissions from soil, fertilizer application, and machinery.

7. **Biodiversity Impact**: Presence of pollinators, pest populations, and crop diversity.

8. **Water Sources and Quality**: Irrigation sources, rainwater harvesting, and runoff quality.

9. **Waste Management**: Composting methods, waste produced, and disposal practices.

10. **Transportation**: Distances and modes for transporting inputs and outputs.

Collect data over multiple growing seasons for accuracy and consider local climate and geographical conditions. Use software tools for modeling and analysis.
