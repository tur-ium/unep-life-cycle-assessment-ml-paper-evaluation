The **functional unit** in Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) defines the *performance* of a product system. It's a quantified description of the service a product delivers – what it *does*, not just what it *is*. 

Crucially, it provides a basis for comparing different products or systems that fulfill the *same* function. For example, "providing 1000km of transportation" or "washing one load of laundry" – allowing for a fair "apples to apples" comparison of environmental impacts.
