For an LCA of a crop from a regenerative farm, essential data includes:

1. **Crop Details**: Type, variety, and specific strain to assess growth patterns and inputs.

2. **Farm Characteristics**: Size, location, soil type, and sustainable practices like crop rotation or cover cropping.

3. **Inputs**: Fertilizers, pesticides, irrigation sources, application rates, and water usage.

4. **Energy Use**: Machinery type (electric/diesel) and fuel consumption to evaluate emissions.

5. **Post-Harvest Handling**: Transportation methods, storage facilities, and processing techniques affecting impact.

6. **Economic Factors**: Production costs including labor, seeds, and inputs for feasibility analysis.

7. **Market Information**: Buyers, sales locations, and transportation distances impacting supply chain emissions.

8. **Waste Management**: Data on waste generation, composting, and recycling practices.

9. **Regenerative Practices**: Documentation of techniques like organic amendments and reduced tillage.

10. **End-of-Life Disposal**: Methods used for crop disposal to evaluate sustainability.

11. **Timing and Environmental Context**: Planting/harvest seasons and regional impacts like water scarcity or biodiversity.

12. **Pest Control**: Methods used, whether chemical or biological, affecting environmental impact.

This structured approach ensures comprehensive assessment of the crop's life cycle from field to market.
