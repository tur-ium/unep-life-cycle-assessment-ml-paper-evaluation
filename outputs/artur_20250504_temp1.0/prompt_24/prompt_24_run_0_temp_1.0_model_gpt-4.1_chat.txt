To conduct an LCA for a crop from a regenerative farm, I need to collect primary data on:

- **Inputs:** Types and quantities of seeds, organic amendments (compost, manure), fertilizers (including source and application rates), and pest/disease control measures (biological or chemical).
- **Energy Use:** Fuels and electricity used for cultivation, irrigation, harvest, and post-harvest processes.
- **Water Use:** Irrigation amounts, source of water.
- **Field Operations:** Details of machinery use (type, hours of operation), tillage practices, and other field interventions (cover cropping, grazing, rotation schedules).
- **Yields:** Quantity of crop harvested per hectare (fresh and dry basis).
- **Emissions:** Direct field emissions (N₂O, CH₄, CO₂), including measurement or estimation of nitrous oxide from soils, methane (if paddy rice or livestock integration), ammonia volatilization, and leaching/runoff.
- **Soil Carbon:** Changes in soil organic carbon stocks, to capture potential sequestration due to regenerative practices.
- **Residue Management:** Treatment of crop residues and by-products.
- **Land Use:** Area cultivated and duration, land use change (if any).
- **Waste Management:** Disposal or recycling of wastes, including plastics or packaging.

Secondary data may be used for background processes (e.g., upstream input production, transport). Collecting qualitative information on regenerative practices (e.g., agroforestry, intercropping) is also important for context and impact interpretation.
