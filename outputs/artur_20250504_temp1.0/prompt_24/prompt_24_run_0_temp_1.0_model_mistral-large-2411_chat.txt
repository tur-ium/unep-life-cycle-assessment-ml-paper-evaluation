To perform a Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) for a crop from a regenerative farm, you'll need to collect data across several categories:

1. **Farm Operations:**
   - Types and quantities of inputs (seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, water)
   - Energy use (fuel, electricity) for machinery, irrigation, and other processes
   - Labor hours and equipment used for cultivation, harvesting, and post-harvest operations

2. **Soil Health:**
   - Soil organic carbon changes
   - Soil nutrient levels and microbial activity
   - Erosion and compaction rates

3. **Biodiversity:**
   - Species diversity and abundance (both plants and animals)
   - Habitat types and quality

4. **Yield and Quality:**
   - Crop yields (tons/hectare)
   - Produce quality and nutrient content

5. **Emissions and Waste:**
   - Greenhouse gas emissions (CO2, N2O, CH4)
   - Other air emissions (NOx, SOx, particulate matter)
   - Water emissions (nitrate, phosphate, pesticide residues)
   - Solid waste generation (crop residues, packaging waste)

6. **Socio-economic:**
   - Farmer and farmworker income
   - Local employment and economic development
   - Community engagement and support

7. **Supply Chain:**
   - Transportation distances and modes
   - Storage and processing methods
   - Packaging materials and waste
