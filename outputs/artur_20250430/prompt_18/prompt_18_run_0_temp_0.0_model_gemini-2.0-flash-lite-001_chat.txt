This BoM provides a breakdown of a toothbrush's components and their environmental impacts. The data includes estimated emissions to air, water, and soil, energy and water use, and the producing processes. The values are estimates and include uncertainties, reflecting the variability inherent in manufacturing and supply chains. This information is crucial for conducting a comprehensive Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) of the toothbrush.
