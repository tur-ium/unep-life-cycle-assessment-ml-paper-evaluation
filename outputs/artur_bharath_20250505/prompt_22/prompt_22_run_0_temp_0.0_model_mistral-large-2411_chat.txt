To determine which product is more environmentally friendly, we need to consider the impacts of each environmental indicator: carbon footprint, water footprint, and marine eutrophication.

### Product A:
- **Carbon Footprint**: 5 kg CO2 eq
- **Water Footprint**: 10 m³ deprived
- **Marine Eutrophication Impact**: 3 kg N eq

### Product B:
- **Carbon Footprint**: 2 kg CO2 eq
- **Water Footprint**: 30 m³ deprived
- **Marine Eutrophication Impact**: 3 kg N eq

### Analysis:

1. **Carbon Footprint**:
   - Product A: 5 kg CO2 eq
   - Product B: 2 kg CO2 eq
   - **Comparison**: Product B has a significantly lower carbon footprint, which is beneficial for reducing greenhouse gas emissions and mitigating climate change.

2. **Water Footprint**:
   - Product A: 10 m³ deprived
   - Product B: 30 m³ deprived
   - **Comparison**: Product A has a much lower water footprint, which is important for water conservation and reducing stress on freshwater resources.

3. **Marine Eutrophication Impact**:
   - Both products have the same marine eutrophication impact of 3 kg N eq.
   - **Comparison**: There is no difference between the two products in terms of marine eutrophication impact.

### Conclusion:

- **Product A** is more environmentally friendly in terms of water footprint.
- **Product B** is more environmentally friendly in terms of carbon footprint.
- Both products are equal in terms of marine eutrophication impact.

Given that both products have the same marine eutrophication impact, the decision comes down to prioritizing between carbon footprint and water footprint. If reducing greenhouse gas emissions is the primary concern, **Product B** is more environmentally friendly. If water conservation is the primary concern, **Product A** is more environmentally friendly.

In many environmental assessments, reducing greenhouse gas emissions is often given higher priority due to the global impact of climate change. Therefore, **Product B** might be considered more environmentally friendly overall, despite its higher water footprint. However, the final choice may depend on specific contexts and priorities.
