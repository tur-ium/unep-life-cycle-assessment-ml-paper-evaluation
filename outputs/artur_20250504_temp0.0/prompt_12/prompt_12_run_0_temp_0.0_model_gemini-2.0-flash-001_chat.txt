No, you cannot directly use ReCiPe 2016 or CML-IA for Social Life Cycle Assessment (S-LCA).

Here's why:

*   **ReCiPe 2016 and CML-IA are designed for Environmental Life Cycle Assessment (E-LCA).** They focus on environmental impacts like climate change, resource depletion, and toxicity. They use environmental indicators and characterization factors to translate emissions and resource use into environmental impact scores.

*   **S-LCA focuses on social and socioeconomic impacts.** It considers the impacts on stakeholders like workers, local communities, consumers, and society as a whole. It looks at issues like human rights, working conditions, health and safety, cultural heritage, and governance.

*   **Different Indicators and Data:** E-LCA and S-LCA use fundamentally different indicators and data. E-LCA relies on quantitative data about emissions, resource use, and energy consumption. S-LCA often relies on qualitative and semi-quantitative data about social conditions, stakeholder perceptions, and policy implementation.

*   **Different Methodologies:** While both E-LCA and S-LCA follow a similar framework (goal and scope definition, inventory analysis, impact assessment, interpretation), the specific methods used for impact assessment are very different. E-LCA uses characterization factors to convert inventory data into environmental impact scores. S-LCA uses various methods, including qualitative assessments, quantitative indicators, and stakeholder engagement, to assess social impacts.

**What to use for S-LCA:**

Instead of ReCiPe 2016 or CML-IA, you should use a dedicated S-LCA methodology. Some common S-LCA methodologies include:

*   **Guidelines for Social Life Cycle Assessment of Products (UNEP/SETAC):** This is the most widely recognized and comprehensive guidance document for S-LCA. It provides a framework for conducting S-LCA and includes a list of social impact categories and indicators.
*   **PSILCA database:** This database provides information on social hotspots and potential social impacts associated with different products and processes.
*   **Other S-LCA frameworks:** Various researchers and organizations have developed their own S-LCA frameworks and indicators.

**In summary:**

ReCiPe 2016 and CML-IA are excellent tools for E-LCA, but they are not suitable for S-LCA. You need to use a dedicated S-LCA methodology and appropriate social indicators to assess the social impacts of a product or service.
