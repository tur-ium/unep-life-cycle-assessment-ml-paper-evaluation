The carbon footprint of producing a high-end consumer laptop is typically in the range of **200–400 kg CO₂e** per device.

### Explanation and Sources

- **Teehan & Kandlikar (2013)** estimated the embodied emissions of a 2009 MacBook Pro at **270 kg CO₂e**.
- **Apple (2023)** reports for its latest MacBook Pro models (14-inch and 16-inch, M2 Pro/Max) a **lifecycle carbon footprint of 340–400 kg CO₂e**, with **70–80%** of that from production (i.e., **~240–320 kg CO₂e**).
- **Deng et al. (2011)** found that the manufacturing of a typical laptop (not necessarily high-end) results in **210–270 kg CO₂e**.

The majority of emissions come from the extraction and processing of raw materials, manufacturing of components (especially the display and logic board), and assembly.

### Calculation Example

For a high-end laptop (e.g., MacBook Pro 16-inch, 2023):
- **Total lifecycle emissions:** 400 kg CO₂e (Apple, 2023)
- **Production share:** ~75% (Apple, 2023)
- **Production emissions:** 0.75 × 400 = **300 kg CO₂e**

### **Final Output**

**The carbon footprint of producing a high-end consumer laptop is approximately 300 kg CO₂e.**
