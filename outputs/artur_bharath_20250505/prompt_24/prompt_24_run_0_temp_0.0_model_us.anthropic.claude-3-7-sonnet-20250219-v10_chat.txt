# Data Needed for LCA of a Regenerative Farm Crop

To conduct a life cycle assessment (LCA) for a crop from a regenerative farm, I would need to collect:

**Farm Inputs:**
- Seed quantities and sources
- Water usage (irrigation amounts, sources)
- Energy consumption (electricity, fuel for machinery)
- Fertilizer applications (organic amendments, compost quantities)
- Pest management inputs (biological controls, minimal pesticides if used)

**Farm Management:**
- Tillage practices (reduced/no-till methods)
- Cover cropping details (species, biomass production)
- Crop rotation schedules
- Livestock integration data (if applicable)

**Farm Outputs:**
- Crop yields
- Co-products and their uses
- Waste generation and management

**Carbon and Environmental Metrics:**
- Soil organic carbon measurements (before/after)
- Soil health indicators (biodiversity, structure)
- GHG emissions from operations
- Land use changes
- Biodiversity impacts

**Processing and Distribution:**
- Post-harvest processing energy and resources
- Transportation distances and modes
- Packaging materials and quantities

**Temporal Data:**
- Changes in soil carbon sequestration over time
- Multi-year yield data to account for regenerative transition

This data allows for comparison with conventional agriculture while capturing the unique benefits of regenerative practices, particularly regarding carbon sequestration, biodiversity enhancement, and reduced external inputs.
