**Explanation:**  
Life Cycle Assessment (LCA), as defined in ISO 14044, is a systematic method for evaluating the environmental impacts associated with all the stages of a product's life—from raw material extraction through production, use, and disposal. LCA is particularly suited to quantifying impacts such as carbon footprint, energy use, and resource depletion for products or processes.

Option **C** ("What is the impact of using a new synthesis route for a feedstock chemical on the carbon footprint of a plastic sheet?") directly aligns with the purpose of LCA. It asks about the environmental impact (specifically, carbon footprint) of a change in the production process of a material used in a product. This is a classic LCA application: comparing the environmental impacts of alternative production routes or materials.

The other options (A, B, D) focus on policy comparison, medical outcomes, or biological life cycles, none of which are directly addressed by LCA as defined in ISO 14044. Thus, **C** is the best fit.
