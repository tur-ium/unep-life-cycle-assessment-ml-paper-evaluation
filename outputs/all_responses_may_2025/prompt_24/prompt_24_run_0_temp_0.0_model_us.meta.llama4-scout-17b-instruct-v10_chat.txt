As an LCA (Life Cycle Assessment) practitioner working on an LCA for a crop from a regenerative farm, I would need to collect data on the following:

1. **Farm-level data**:
	* Soil type, climate, and location
	* Crop type, yield, and rotation
	* Farming practices (e.g., tillage, irrigation, fertilization, pest management)
	* Livestock and biodiversity management (if applicable)
2. **Input data**:
	* Seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, and other agricultural inputs used
	* Energy consumption (e.g., fuel, electricity) for farming operations
	* Water usage and management
	* Transportation of inputs and outputs
3. **Output data**:
	* Crop yield and quality
	* Soil health indicators (e.g., organic matter, carbon sequestration)
	* Biodiversity metrics (e.g., species richness, ecosystem services)
4. **Emissions and removals**:
	* Greenhouse gas emissions (e.g., N2O, CO2, CH4) from farming operations
	* Carbon sequestration in soils and biomass
5. **End-of-life data**:
	* Harvesting and post-harvest handling practices
	* Transportation to processing, storage, and distribution
	* Packaging and end-product use

This data will help me assess the environmental impacts of the regenerative farm's crop production, from cradle to gate, and identify areas for improvement.
