To conduct an LCA for a crop from a regenerative farm, key data include:  
1. **Input materials**: Seed type, organic/compost fertilizers, pesticides (if any), and water sources.  
2. **Energy use**: Fuel for machinery, renewable energy sources, and irrigation systems.  
3. **Emissions**: Greenhouse gases (CO₂, N₂O, CH₄), soil carbon sequestration rates, and air pollutants.  
4. **Land use**: Soil health metrics (organic matter, erosion), biodiversity indicators, and land management practices (e.g., cover cropping, no-till).  
5. **Water use**: Irrigation volumes, rainfall reliance, and water quality impacts.  
6. **Transportation**: Distance and mode of transport to market, packaging materials.  
7. **Processing**: Energy and water use during post-harvest handling.  
8. **Waste**: Composting, byproduct utilization, and landfill contributions.  
9. **System boundaries**: Define scope (e.g., cradle-to-farm-gate or cradle-to-grave).  
10. **Comparative data**: Baseline metrics for conventional farming to highlight regenerative benefits.  
Prioritize data on regenerative practices (e.g., carbon farming, agroecology) and their environmental impacts. Ensure transparency in data sources and assumptions for accuracy.
