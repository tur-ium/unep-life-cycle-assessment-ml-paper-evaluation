In a slaughter process, the **sales value at split-off method** is the most appropriate multi-output allocation method. This approach allocates joint costs based on the relative market values of the products (e.g., meat, hides, offal) at the point where they are separated. It ensures costs are distributed proportionally to the revenue-generating potential of each output, aligning with economic reality. 

Using this method is fair because higher-value products (e.g., premium cuts) naturally incur a larger share of costs, reflecting their contribution to profitability. Alternatives like physical measures (e.g., weight) are less suitable, as they ignore value disparities (e.g., hides may weigh less but have significant market value). The sales value method also supports accurate cost analysis, pricing decisions, and profitability assessments, which are critical in industries with diverse by-products like slaughter.
