**Recommendation for LCA Methodology Selection**

The choice between Attributional (ALCA) and Consequential (CLCA) LCA depends critically on the study's goal, the scale of the process, and the decisions it aims to inform.

1.  **Pilot-scale Demonstration Phase:**
    *   **Recommended Approach:** Attributional LCA (ALCA).
    *   **Goal & Scope:** To understand the environmental profile and technical hotspots of the *specific pilot unit* under defined operating conditions. The scope should be cradle-to-gate (hydrogen production).
    *   **Data Collection:** Prioritize primary data directly from the pilot facility (energy, materials, emissions). Use average data for upstream inputs (e.g., solar panel manufacturing) where primary data is unavailable.
    *   **Justification:** At this stage, the goal is internal improvement and baseline assessment, not predicting market shifts (Finnveden et al., 2009). Stakeholders (R&D, internal management) need to understand the *intrinsic* performance of *this* technology. CLCA is unnecessarily complex as the pilot scale has negligible market consequences.
