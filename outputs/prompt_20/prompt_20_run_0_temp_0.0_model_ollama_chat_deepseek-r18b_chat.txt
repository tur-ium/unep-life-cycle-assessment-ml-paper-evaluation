To address storing carbon in orange trees within a Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) study, follow these steps:

1. **Identify Key Stages**: Examine processes such as growth, transportation, consumption, and disposal, each contributing to carbon storage.

2. **Quantify Carbon Storage**: Use data on factors like yield per tree, transportation methods, and waste generation rates to estimate the amount of carbon stored at each stage.

3. **Assess Environmental Impact**: Compare the stored carbon across different scenarios, such as local vs. long-distance transportation, to evaluate emissions contributions.

4. **Evaluate Trade-offs**: Utilize life cycle costing tools to assess trade-offs between stages, balancing initial costs with long-term environmental benefits.

5. **Consider Tree Disposal**: Determine how the tree's disposal affects carbon release, whether through replanting or waste management practices.

By systematically addressing these aspects, the study provides insights for reducing the carbon footprint of orange production, aiding in more sustainable decision-making.
