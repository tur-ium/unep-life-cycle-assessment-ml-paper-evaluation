As an LCA (Life Cycle Assessment) practitioner focusing on a crop from a regenerative farm, you'll need to collect data on various aspects to ensure a comprehensive analysis. Key data points include:

1. **Agricultural Practices**: Details on tillage methods, crop rotation, cover cropping, and integrated pest management.
2. **Inputs**: Quantities of seeds, fertilizers (organic and synthetic), pesticides, and water used.
3. **Energy Use**: Fuel consumption for machinery, electricity for irrigation and other farm operations.
4. **Soil Health**: Data on soil organic matter, carbon sequestration, and nutrient levels.
5. **Yield Data**: Crop yields and quality metrics.
6. **Waste Management**: Handling of agricultural waste, composting practices, and wastewater treatment.
7. **Biodiversity**: Information on local biodiversity, including beneficial insects, birds, and plant species.
8. **Transportation**: Distances and modes of transport for inputs and outputs.
9. **Emissions**: Greenhouse gas emissions from farming activities, including nitrous oxide from soil and methane from livestock if applicable.
10. **Social and Economic Factors**: Labor practices, economic viability, and community impacts.

Collecting this data will help you assess the environmental, social, and economic impacts of the regenerative farming practices, providing a holistic view of the crop's life cycle.
