As an LCA (Life Cycle Assessment) practitioner focusing on a crop from a regenerative farm, you'll need to collect data on various aspects to ensure a comprehensive analysis. Key data points include:

1. **Agricultural Practices**: Details on tillage methods, crop rotation, cover cropping, and integrated pest management.
2. **Inputs**: Quantities of seeds, fertilizers (organic and synthetic), pesticides, and water used.
3. **Energy Use**: Fuel consumption for machinery, electricity for irrigation and other farm operations.
4. **Soil Health**: Data on soil organic matter, carbon sequestration, and nutrient levels.
5. **Yield Data**: Crop yields over time to assess productivity.
6. **Biodiversity**: Information on local flora and fauna, including any beneficial insects or pollinators.
7. **Waste Management**: Handling of agricultural waste, composting practices, and any waste-to-energy initiatives.
8. **Transportation**: Distances and modes of transport for inputs and outputs, including to markets or processing facilities.
9. **Emissions**: Greenhouse gas emissions from farming activities, including nitrous oxide from fertilizers and methane from any livestock.
10. **Social and Economic Factors**: Labor practices, community engagement, and economic viability of the farm.

Collecting this data will help you accurately assess the environmental, social, and economic impacts of the regenerative farming practices.
