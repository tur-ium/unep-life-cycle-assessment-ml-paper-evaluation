The question in option C asks about the impact of a new synthesis route on the carbon footprint of a plastic sheet, which directly aligns with the purpose of Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) under ISO 14044. LCA evaluates environmental impacts (e.g., carbon emissions) across a product’s entire life cycle, from raw material extraction to disposal. By comparing the environmental effects of different production methods (e.g., a new vs. old synthesis route), LCA quantifies changes in impacts like greenhouse gas emissions, making it the ideal tool for this scenario.
