

To conduct a Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) for a crop from a regenerative farm, I need to collect data on various inputs and outputs across the entire life cycle of the crop. This includes:

1. **Agricultural phase**: Data on seeds, fertilizers (synthetic and organic), pesticides, irrigation water, energy consumption for farm operations, and soil management practices (e.g., tillage, cover cropping).
2. **Crop yields and management**: Information on crop yields, harvesting methods, and any post-harvest treatments.
3. **Regenerative practices**: Details on regenerative agriculture practices implemented, such as no-till or reduced-till farming, cover cropping, crop rotation, organic amendments, and integrated pest management.
4. **Inputs and emissions**: Quantities of inputs like fuels, lubricants, and electricity used, as well as emissions from farm operations (e.g., N2O from soils, CO2 from machinery).
5. **Transportation and storage**: Data on transportation modes and distances for inputs and the harvested crop, as well as storage conditions and energy use.
6. **End-of-life**: Information on the crop's end-use (e.g., food, feed, bioenergy) and any waste generated during processing or consumption.

This data will help assess the environmental impacts of the regenerative farm's crop production, such as greenhouse gas emissions, water use, and land use, and compare it to conventional farming practices.
