## 3: Define the scope of the LCA.
The scope of the LCA should include:
- **System boundaries**: The assessment should consider the entire life cycle of each land use option, from construction/installation (or land preparation for agriculture) to decommissioning/end of life. For solar farms, this includes material extraction, manufacturing of solar panels, installation, operation, and recycling or disposal at the end of life. For agriculture and agrivoltaics, it includes farming practices, input production (e.g., fertilizers, pesticides), and potential land use changes.
- **Impact categories**: In addition to greenhouse gas emissions (global warming potential), the assessment should consider other relevant environmental impact categories such as land use, water use, biodiversity loss, and potential soil degradation or improvement.
- **Functional unit**: An appropriate functional unit could be "1 hectare of land use over 20 years" or "1 MWh of electricity generated," allowing for a comparison of the environmental performance of different land use options on an equal basis.
- **Geographical scope**: The assessment should focus on the local and regional environmental impacts, given the community's concerns about jobs, tax revenue, and local ecosystem effects, in addition to broader, global impacts like climate change.
