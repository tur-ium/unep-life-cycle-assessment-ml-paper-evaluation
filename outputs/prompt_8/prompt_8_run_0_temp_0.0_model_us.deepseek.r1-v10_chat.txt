**Methodological Strategy for LCA of Battery Recycling vs. Reuse**  
*Handling Data Uncertainty & Technological Variability*  
To address data gaps in reuse scenarios, **proxy data** from analogous second-life applications (e.g., repurposed EV batteries in grid storage) and engineering models (e.g., degradation rates, cycle life) will be synthesized, with uncertainty quantified via **pedigree matrices** (Weidema & Wesnæs, 1996). For evolving recycling technologies, **scenario analysis** (e.g., hydrometallurgical vs. direct recycling) and **Monte Carlo simulations** will assess variability, while **sensitivity analysis** identifies critical parameters (Lloyd & Ries, 2007). Temporal dynamics are addressed using technology forecasts from industry roadmaps (e.g., IRENA, 2023) and expert elicitation.  

*Boundaries & Functional Unit*  
**System boundaries** include:  
- **Recycling**: Collection, transportation, material recovery, and new battery production.  
- **Reuse**: Repurposing (testing, module replacement), secondary storage use, and eventual recycling.  
Excluded processes (e.g., mining for virgin materials) are justified via cut-off criteria (ISO 14044).  

The **functional unit** is *1 MWh of delivered energy storage over 20 years*, accounting for degradation and cycle life differences. This ensures equivalence in service provision (Ekvall & Tillman, 1997). **System expansion** avoids allocation for multi-functional processes (e.g., avoided virgin material production in recycling).  

*Justifying Assumptions to Stakeholders*  
1. **Transparency**: Document data sources (e.g., proxy assumptions for reuse from Ahmadi et al., 2017), uncertainty ranges, and exclusion rationales.  
2. **Visualization**: Use flow diagrams to clarify boundaries and Sankey charts for material/energy flows.  
3. **Benchmarking**: Compare methods to established LCAs (Olivetti et al., 2013) and standards (ISO 14040).  
4. **Stakeholder Engagement**: Workshops to explain trade-offs (e.g., higher initial reuse emissions vs. long-term recycling benefits) using simplified sensitivity tornado plots.

This approach balances rigor with accessibility, enabling informed decision-making despite data limitations.
