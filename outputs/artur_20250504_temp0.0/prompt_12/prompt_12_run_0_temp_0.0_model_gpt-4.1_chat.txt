**No, you should not use ReCiPe 2016 or CML-IA for social life cycle assessment (S-LCA).**

**Here’s why:**

- **ReCiPe 2016** (Huijbregts et al., 2016) and **CML-IA** (developed by the Centrum voor Milieukunde Leiden) are both **environmental life cycle impact assessment (LCIA) methods**. They are designed to assess and characterize **environmental impacts** such as climate change, acidification, eutrophication, human toxicity (from an environmental perspective), resource depletion, etc.
- These methods **do not include social indicators** or social impact categories. Their impact categories are strictly environmental and do not cover social issues like labor rights, human health from a social perspective, child labor, community impacts, or other social aspects.

**For Social LCA:**
- Social LCA uses **different indicators and methods**. The most widely recognized framework is the **UNEP/SETAC Guidelines for Social Life Cycle Assessment of Products** (2009, 2020), which recommend using social impact categories such as human rights, working conditions, health and safety, cultural heritage, etc.
- There are specific **social impact assessment methods and databases** (e.g., PSILCA, Social Hotspots Database) designed for S-LCA.

**Summary Table:**

| Method         | Type of LCA | Impact Categories Covered         | Suitable for S-LCA? |
|----------------|-------------|-----------------------------------|---------------------|
| ReCiPe 2016    | Environmental| Environmental only                | No                  |
| CML-IA         | Environmental| Environmental only                | No                  |
| S-LCA (UNEP/SETAC, PSILCA, etc.) | Social       | Social (human rights, labor, etc.) | Yes                 |

**References:**
- Huijbregts, M.A.J., et al. (2016). ReCiPe2016: a harmonised life cycle impact assessment method at midpoint and endpoint level. *International Journal of Life Cycle Assessment*, 22(2), 138-147.
- UNEP/SETAC (2009, 2020). Guidelines for Social Life Cycle Assessment of Products.

**In summary:**  
**ReCiPe 2016 and CML-IA are not suitable for social LCA. Use S-LCA-specific methods and indicators for social impact assessment.**
