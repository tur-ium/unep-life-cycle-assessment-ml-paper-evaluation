### Explanation  
A typical plastic toothbrush’s environmental impact arises from fossil-based polymers (handle, bristles), energy-intensive processes (molding, extrusion), and microplastic pollution. Cardboard packaging reduces plastic waste but increases water use. Emissions include CO₂, VOCs, and microplastics, with disposal-phase soil contamination. Energy and water use vary by material and process efficiency.
