The carbon footprint of producing a high-end consumer laptop typically ranges between approximately 200 to 300 kg CO₂e per unit. This estimate accounts for the extraction and processing of raw materials, manufacturing processes, assembly, and transportation. For example, a study by Ellingsen et al. (2016) estimates that the production phase of a laptop contributes around 200 kg CO₂e, considering the energy-intensive manufacturing of components such as the display, battery, and circuit boards. Additional factors, such as the sourcing of rare materials and the energy mix used in factories, can influence this figure (Hertwich et al., 2015). Therefore, a reasonable average estimate for a high-end laptop's production carbon footprint is approximately **250 kg CO₂e**.
