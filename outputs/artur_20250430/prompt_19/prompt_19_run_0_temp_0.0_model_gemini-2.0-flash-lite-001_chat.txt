The most appropriate multi-output allocation method for a slaughter process is **physical measures (e.g., weight or volume)**.

Here's why:

*   **Direct Relationship:** Physical measures like carcass weight or the volume of byproducts (blood, offal) directly reflect the physical output of the slaughter process.
*   **Cost Driver:** The amount of resources consumed (labor, utilities, etc.) often correlates with the physical quantity of the outputs. Larger animals or more byproducts generally require more resources.
*   **Simplicity:** This method is relatively easy to implement and understand, making it practical for a complex process.
*   **Fairness:** It provides a more equitable allocation of costs compared to methods that might arbitrarily assign costs based on sales value or other less direct factors.

Using physical measures ensures costs are allocated based on the actual resources consumed in producing each output.
