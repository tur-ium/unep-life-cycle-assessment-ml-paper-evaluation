To determine which product is more environmentally friendly, we need to consider the impacts of each environmental indicator: carbon footprint, water footprint, and marine eutrophication.

### Product A:
- **Carbon Footprint**: 5 kg CO2 eq
- **Water Footprint**: 10 m³ deprived
- **Marine Eutrophication Impact**: 3 kg N eq

### Product B:
- **Carbon Footprint**: 2 kg CO2 eq
- **Water Footprint**: 30 m³ deprived
- **Marine Eutrophication Impact**: 3 kg N eq

### Analysis:

1. **Carbon Footprint**:
   - Product A: 5 kg CO2 eq
   - Product B: 2 kg CO2 eq
   - **Comparison**: Product B has a significantly lower carbon footprint, which is beneficial for reducing greenhouse gas emissions and mitigating climate change.

2. **Water Footprint**:
   - Product A: 10 m³ deprived
   - Product B: 30 m³ deprived
   - **Comparison**: Product A has a much lower water footprint, which is important for conserving freshwater resources and reducing water scarcity.

3. **Marine Eutrophication Impact**:
   - Product A: 3 kg N eq
   - Product B: 3 kg N eq
   - **Comparison**: Both products have the same marine eutrophication impact, meaning they contribute equally to the nutrient pollution of water bodies.

### Conclusion:

- **Product A** is more environmentally friendly in terms of water footprint.
- **Product B** is more environmentally friendly in terms of carbon footprint.
- Both products are equally impactful in terms of marine eutrophication.

Given that carbon footprint is often considered a critical factor in environmental impact due to its direct link to climate change, **Product B** might be considered more environmentally friendly overall. However, the choice could depend on the specific context and priorities. For example, in regions with severe water scarcity, the water footprint might be more critical, making **Product A** the better choice.

In summary, **Product B** is generally more environmentally friendly due to its lower carbon footprint, but the final decision should consider the specific environmental priorities and context.
