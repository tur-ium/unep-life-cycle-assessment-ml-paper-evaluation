**Explanation:**

According to ISO 15804:2012+A2:2019, which is the standard for Environmental Product Declarations (EPD) for construction products (harmonized with ISO 14044), the recommended hierarchy for resolving multi-functionality is:

1. **First, avoid allocation by subdivision or system expansion (subsystem or system boundary modification)**  
2. **If allocation is unavoidable, use physical relationships (such as mass, energy content, or other relevant physical properties)**
3. **If physical relationships cannot be established, use another relationship such as economic value (i.e., allocation on basis of revenue)**

This mirrors the hierarchy in ISO 14044, and the intent is always to **avoid allocation where possible**.

So, the correct numbered order is:
1. **Avoid allocation as far as possible**
2. **Allocation on basis of physical properties**
3. **Allocation on basis of revenue**
