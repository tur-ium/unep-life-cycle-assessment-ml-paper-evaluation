# ReCiPe 2016 and CML-IA for Social LCA

No, neither ReCiPe 2016 nor CML-IA are appropriate methodologies for Social Life Cycle Assessment (S-LCA). These are specifically designed for environmental Life Cycle Assessment (E-LCA) and focus on environmental impact categories.

## Why these methods don't work for S-LCA:

- **ReCiPe 2016** (Huijbregts et al., 2016) focuses on environmental midpoint and endpoint indicators like climate change, ozone depletion, and resource scarcity.

- **CML-IA** from the Centrum voor Milieukunde Leiden similarly addresses environmental impact categories like global warming potential and acidification.

## For Social LCA, you should instead use:

1. **UNEP/SETAC Guidelines for S-LCA** - The foundational methodology specifically developed for social impacts

2. **Social Hotspots Database (SHDB)** methodology

3. **Product Social Impact Life Cycle Assessment (PSILCA)** database and methodology

4. **Social Impact Weighting Method**

5. **Handbook for Product Social Impact Assessment**

These S-LCA methodologies incorporate appropriate social indicators like labor rights, health and safety, cultural heritage, and governance that aren't addressed in environmental LCA methods.
