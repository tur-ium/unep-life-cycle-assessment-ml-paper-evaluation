# Comparing Climate Change Impact: 5 kg CO2 vs 1 kg N2O using EF 3.1

To determine which emission has more impact on climate change according to the Environmental Footprint (EF) 3.1 impact assessment method, I need to compare their Global Warming Potentials (GWPs).

## EF 3.1 Characterization Factors

Under the EF 3.1 method published by the European Commission:
- CO2 has a GWP of 1 kg CO2-eq/kg (reference substance)
- N2O has a GWP of 273 kg CO2-eq/kg (based on IPCC AR5 with climate-carbon feedbacks)

## Calculation

For 5 kg CO2:
5 kg CO2 × 1 kg CO2-eq/kg = 5 kg CO2-eq

For 1 kg N2O:
1 kg N2O × 273 kg CO2-eq/kg = 273 kg CO2-eq

## Comparison and Explanation

**1 kg of N2O has significantly more impact on climate change than 5 kg of CO2** (273 kg CO2-eq vs. 5 kg CO2-eq).

This large difference occurs because N2O is a much more potent greenhouse gas than CO2. The higher GWP value for N2O reflects:

1. Its greater efficiency at absorbing infrared radiation
2. Its longer atmospheric lifetime (approximately 114 years compared to CO2's variable lifetime)
3. Its impact on stratospheric ozone

Therefore, even though the mass of CO2 is 5 times greater in this comparison, the climate change impact of 1 kg of N2O is approximately 54.6 times greater than that of 5 kg of CO2 according to the EF 3.1 methodology.
