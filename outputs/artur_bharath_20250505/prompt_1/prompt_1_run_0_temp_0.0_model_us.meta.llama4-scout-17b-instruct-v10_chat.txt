## Step 1: Understand the life cycle assessment (LCA) stages
A life cycle assessment (LCA) is a method used to evaluate the environmental impacts of a product throughout its entire life cycle. The stages of LCA typically include extracting and processing raw materials, manufacturing and packaging, use and operation during lifetime, and disposal at the end of useful life.

## 2: Identify the stage related to biodegradability and recyclability
Biodegradability and recyclability are characteristics that relate to what happens to a product at the end of its useful life. This includes how the product can be disposed of or reused in an environmentally friendly manner.

## 3: Match the information with the correct LCA stage
- A: Disposal at the end of useful life: This stage involves the end-of-life management of a product, including recycling, biodegradation, or other disposal methods.
- B: Extracting and processing raw materials: This stage is about obtaining and processing the raw materials needed for the product.
- C: Manufacturing and packaging: This stage involves the creation and packaging of the product.
- D: Use and operation during lifetime: This stage is about the environmental impacts during the product's use.

## 4: Select the correct stage
Given that biodegradability and recyclability pertain to the product's fate after it has served its purpose, this information would be most relevant during the stage of disposal at the end of useful life.

The best answer is A.
