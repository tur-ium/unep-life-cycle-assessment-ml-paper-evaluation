This BoM represents a typical manual toothbrush with plastic handle, nylon bristles and rubber grip components. The environmental impact is dominated by plastic production processes and associated emissions. The total lifecycle energy consumption is approximately 4.0 MJ per toothbrush, with water usage around 2.0 L. Transportation impacts vary significantly based on supply chain geography.
