**Steps to Harmonize Findings**  
1. **Standardize Functional Units**: Align studies by converting results to a common basis (e.g., per kg of product or per 100 kcal) to ensure comparability.  
2. **Compare System Boundaries**: Identify gaps (e.g., inclusion of upstream/downstream processes) and adjust analyses to reflect consistent scopes.

5. **Conduct Sensitivity Analyses**: Test the robustness of results to key assumptions (e.g., land-use change, feed efficiency) to identify drivers of discrepancy.
