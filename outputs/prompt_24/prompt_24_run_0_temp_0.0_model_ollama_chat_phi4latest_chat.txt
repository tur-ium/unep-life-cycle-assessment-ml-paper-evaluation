As an LCA practitioner assessing a crop from a regenerative farm, it's crucial to gather comprehensive data across the entire life cycle of the product. Here’s a concise list of essential data points:

1. **Agricultural Practices:**
   - Crop type and variety.
   - Soil management techniques (e.g., cover cropping, composting).
   - Water usage and irrigation methods.
   - Fertilizer and pesticide use, including organic or regenerative inputs.

2. **Land Use Changes:**
   - Historical land use data to assess changes in biodiversity and carbon sequestration.
   - Impact of regenerative practices on soil health (e.g., organic matter content).

3. **Energy Consumption:**
   - Energy sources for farm operations, including renewable energy usage.
   - Fuel consumption for machinery.

4. **Transportation:**
   - Distance from farm to processing and distribution centers.
   - Modes of transportation used.

5. **Processing and Packaging:**
   - Energy and water use in processing facilities.
   - Materials used for packaging and their environmental impact.

6. **Distribution and Retail:**
   - Logistics data, including emissions from transport.
   - Storage conditions and energy consumption at retail locations.

7. **End-of-Life:**
   - Waste management practices (e.g., composting of organic waste).
   - Potential for recycling or reuse of packaging materials.

8. **Biodiversity and Ecosystem Services:**
   - Impact on local biodiversity.
   - Contribution to ecosystem services like pollination, water regulation, etc.

9. **Social and Economic Data:**
   - Labor practices and community impact.
   - Economic benefits to the local area.

10. **Regenerative Metrics:**
    - Carbon sequestration rates.
    - Soil health indicators (e.g., erosion rates, nutrient cycling).

Collecting this data will enable a comprehensive assessment of the environmental impacts associated with regenerative farming practices, highlighting both benefits and areas for improvement.
