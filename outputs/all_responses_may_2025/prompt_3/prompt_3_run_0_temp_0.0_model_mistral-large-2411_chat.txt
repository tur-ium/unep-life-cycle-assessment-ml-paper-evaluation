The question that can be best answered using life cycle assessment (LCA) as defined in the ISO 14044 standard is:

**C: What is the impact of using a new synthesis route for a feedstock chemical on the carbon footprint of a plastic sheet?**

Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) is a methodology for assessing environmental impacts associated with all the stages of a product's life from raw material extraction through materials processing, manufacture, distribution, use, repair and maintenance, and disposal or recycling. ISO 14044 provides the framework for conducting LCA studies.

Option C directly relates to assessing the environmental impact (specifically the carbon footprint) of a change in the production process of a material. This involves evaluating the entire life cycle of the plastic sheet, from the synthesis of the feedstock chemical to the final product, which is precisely what LCA is designed to do.

The other options do not fit the scope of LCA as well:
- **A** involves policy comparison and is more suited to policy analysis.
- **B** focuses on medical outcomes and is better addressed through clinical studies or health impact assessments.
- **D** is about biological life cycles and population control, which is more relevant to ecological or agricultural studies.
