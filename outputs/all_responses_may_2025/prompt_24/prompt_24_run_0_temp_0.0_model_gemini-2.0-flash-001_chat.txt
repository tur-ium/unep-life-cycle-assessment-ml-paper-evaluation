For an LCA of a crop from a regenerative farm, I need data covering the entire life cycle, focusing on regenerative practices. Key data includes:

*   **Farm Inputs:** Seed type/source, fertilizer (type, amount, production data), pesticides/herbicides (type, amount, production data), irrigation water (source, amount, energy for pumping), fuel consumption for machinery (type, amount).
*   **Regenerative Practices:** Cover cropping (species, duration, biomass), tillage practices (type, frequency, depth), crop rotation (sequence, duration), composting/manure application (source, amount, C:N ratio), agroforestry integration (species, density, management).
*   **Field Emissions:** Soil carbon sequestration rate (measured or modeled), N2O emissions, CH4 emissions, NH3 volatilization.
*   **Harvest & Processing:** Yield, harvesting method, transportation distance, processing energy/materials.
*   **Farm Location & Climate:** Soil type, rainfall, temperature, solar radiation.

This data will allow me to quantify the environmental impacts and benefits associated with the regenerative farming system compared to conventional practices.
