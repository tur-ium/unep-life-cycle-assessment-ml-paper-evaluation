The carbon footprint of producing a high-end consumer laptop is typically in the range of **200–350 kg CO₂e** (0.2–0.35 tonnes CO₂e) per device.

**Explanation and sources:**

1. **Apple's Environmental Reports** are among the most transparent: Apple estimates that the production of a new MacBook Pro (14-inch, 2023) emits about **294 kg CO₂e** (Apple 2023). Production represents the largest share of the lifecycle emissions, far more than use, transport, or end-of-life.

2. **Dell's Product Carbon Footprints:** Dell estimates that the manufacturing of its XPS 13 9380 laptop accounts for around **279 kg CO₂e per device** (Dell 2020).

3. **Peer-reviewed meta-studies:** A review by Malmodin and Lundén (2016) shows that **manufacturing dominates the carbon footprint of laptops**, and most modern laptops range from **200–400 kg CO₂e** per unit, confirming industry numbers.

4. **Summary Table:**

**Conclusion:**

> The carbon footprint of producing a high-end consumer laptop is **approximately 0.2–0.35 tonnes CO₂e** per device, based on transparent disclosures from leading manufacturers and independent studies (Apple 2023; Dell 2020; Malmodin & Lundén 2016).
