The following is an example scenario for a life cycle assessment. What is an
appropriate goal and scope definition for this scenario? Please justify your choices.
a small, rural community has been approached by a solar developer to convert agricultural land into a solar farm. 

The developer argues that, because solar power has less greenhouse gas emissions than fossil fuels (Cherp et al., 2021), and because agriculture emits substantial amounts of greenhouse gases (United States Department of Agriculture, 2022), especially nitrous oxide and methane, that undergoing this project will contribute significantly to fighting climate change. 

Other stakeholders may have different opinions and goals, with some in the community focused significantly on ensuring meaningful jobs for its citizens and others interested in the expected increase in tax revenue generated by the project. Some members of the community have proposed a middle path, using agrivoltaics (Dinesh and Pearce, 2016) to gain benefits from both green electricity production while maintaining agricultural productivity and reducing land competition. For the town to come to consensus on which pathway is the correct one to use, environmental assessments of the various possibilities may be conducted and be used as evidence to convince the decision makers to choose a given pathway.
