## Step 1: Understand what a Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) is.
A Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) is a method used to evaluate the environmental impacts of a product throughout its entire life cycle, from raw material extraction to end-of-life disposal or recycling.

The stages mentioned are: Extracting and processing raw materials, Manufacturing and packaging, Use and operation during lifetime, and Disposal at the end of useful life.
