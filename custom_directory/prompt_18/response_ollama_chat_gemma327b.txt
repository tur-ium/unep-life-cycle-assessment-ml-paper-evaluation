**Explanation:**

This BoM details the environmental impacts of a typical toothbrush.  The largest impacts stem from plastic production (handle & bristles) and packaging.  Emissions are categorized by medium, and energy/water use is estimated.  Microplastic pollution is a significant concern throughout the toothbrush's lifecycle.  This data is crucial for conducting a full Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) and identifying areas for improvement, such as using bio-based plastics or reducing packaging.
