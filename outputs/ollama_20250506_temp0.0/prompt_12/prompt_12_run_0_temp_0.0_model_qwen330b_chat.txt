The **ReCiPe 2016** (Huijbregts, 2016) and **CML-IA** (from the Centrum voor Milieukunde) are **environmental life cycle impact assessment (LCIA) methods** designed to evaluate **environmental impacts** such as climate change, resource depletion, and ecosystem damage. They **do not include social impact categories** or indicators, which are central to **social life cycle assessment (S-LCA)**. Here's a detailed breakdown:

---

### **1. ReCiPe 2016 and CML-IA: Environmental Focus**
- **ReCiPe 2016** is a comprehensive environmental LCIA method that includes 18 impact categories (e.g., global warming, water use, human toxicity). It is widely used in environmental LCA but **does not address social impacts** like labor rights, health, or community well-being.
- **CML-IA** (CML 2001, 2011, etc.) is another environmental LCIA method with a focus on environmental endpoints (e.g., eutrophication, ozone depletion). Like ReCiPe, it **lacks social impact indicators**.

---

### **2. Social Life Cycle Assessment (S-LCA)**
S-LCA evaluates **social impacts** across the life cycle of a product or service, such as:
- Labor rights (e.g., child labor, forced labor)
- Health and safety (e.g., occupational hazards)
- Human rights (e.g., discrimination, displacement)
- Community impacts (e.g., access to resources, cultural heritage)

**Key standards and frameworks for S-LCA** include:
- **ISO 21930:2011** (for construction and building materials)
- **SETAC's S-LCA Framework** (2010, 2016)
- **Social Hotspots Database (SHD)** by ILO (for identifying social risks in supply chains)

---

### **3. Can ReCiPe 2016 or CML-IA Be Used for S-LCA?**
**No.** These methods are **not suitable for S-LCA** because:
- They **do not include social impact categories** or indicators.
- S-LCA requires **specific data** (e.g., labor conditions, community engagement) that these methods do not address.

---

### **4. Alternatives for S-LCA**
If you need to assess social impacts, use the following:
- **SETAC's S-LCA Framework**: Provides a structured approach to identifying, assessing, and interpreting social impacts.
- **Social Hotspots Database (SHD)**: A tool to identify social risks in supply chains (e.g., labor rights, health).
- **Custom Social Indicators**: Develop or use sector-specific social metrics (e.g., fair wages, safety standards).
- **Hybrid Approaches**: Combine environmental LCIA (e.g., ReCiPe) with S-LCA to address both environmental and social aspects in a full LCA.

---

### **5. Key Takeaways**
- **ReCiPe 2016 and CML-IA are environmental LCIA methods** and **cannot be used for S-LCA**.
- **S-LCA requires dedicated frameworks** like SETAC or ISO 21930, along with social data and indicators.
- If integrating both environmental and social assessments, use **separate methods** for each (e.g., ReCiPe for environmental impacts and S-LCA for social impacts).
