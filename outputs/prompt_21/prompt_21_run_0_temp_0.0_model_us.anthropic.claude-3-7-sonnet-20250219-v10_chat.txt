# Limitations of Our LCA Comparison

While our Life Cycle Assessment provides valuable insights comparing our product against industry averages, several limitations should be acknowledged:

**Data Quality**: Our analysis relies on industry-average data for competitor products, which may not reflect specific manufacturing practices or recent improvements by individual companies.

**System Boundaries**: The assessment focuses primarily on production and use phases, with less comprehensive data on end-of-life scenarios and potential secondary impacts.

**Geographic Variability**: Environmental impacts vary significantly by region due to different energy grids, transportation networks, and waste management systems, which may not be fully captured.

**Temporal Considerations**: The industry is rapidly evolving, meaning comparative data may become outdated as competitors improve their sustainability practices.

**Methodological Choices**: Impact assessment methods involve subjective weighting of environmental categories, potentially favoring certain impact areas over others.

**Uncertainty Factors**: Some environmental impacts contain inherent uncertainty, particularly regarding long-term effects and emerging concerns not yet fully incorporated into LCA methodologies.

We remain committed to transparent reporting and continuous improvement of our assessment methodologies as better data and practices become available.
